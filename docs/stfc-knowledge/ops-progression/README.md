## Getting rare G4 gas

1. **Mine raw G4 gas to feed the refinery.** The community
   [gas systems list](https://star-trek-fleet-command.1337wiki.com/gas/#g4-gas)
   lists Sotavi (warp 56) and Sadell (warp 58) among the nearest open-space
   G4 gas systems. Confirm route and access in-game. Mining yields raw gas, not
   rare refined gas.
2. **Use the G4 materials refinery when eligible.** Scopely's
   [refinery help](https://scopely.helpshift.com/hc/en/19-star-trek-fleet-command/faq/7038-refinery/)
   lists G4 common at Ops 40, uncommon at 41, rare at 43 and epic at 45.
   The [workbook's Master Chart](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nKsUSGGTa0Q2GW4YeAmiQVOiHSJnmfyu54E0Y9n_keg/edit?gid=495226859#gid=495226859)
   instead marks common at Ops 39, while agreeing on uncommon and rare.
   Verify the common unlock in-game; refinery rewards are not guaranteed to
   meet a particular upgrade target.
3. **Consider DTI conversion if unlocked.** The workbook's
   [Mat/Parts Refineries tab](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nKsUSGGTa0Q2GW4YeAmiQVOiHSJnmfyu54E0Y9n_keg/edit?gid=103903467#gid=103903467)
   lists a regular, token-free G4 converter at DTI HQ level 30 and Ops 40,
   with a two-day cooldown: one chest exchanges 457 uncommon for 128 rare
   of the same material. Scopely's
   [DTI HQ help](https://scopely.helpshift.com/hc/en/19-star-trek-fleet-command/faq/8164-department-of-temporal-investigations-headquarters-building/)
   also identifies the level-30 converter and same-material rarity exchanges,
   but says the building itself requires **Ops 42**. Treat Ops 40 alone as
   insufficient; check the building, conversion rate and cooldown in-game
   before spending uncommon gas.
4. **Check eligible rewards.** Scopely's [Priority 1 Away Teams guide](https://startrekfleetcommand.com/news/away-team-assignments-priority-1-assignments-and-rewards/)
   describes uncommon and rare G3–G6 material rewards depending on the
   assignment and Ops grade. The feature starts at Ops 26 with an Away Teams
   research unlock; inspect each available assignment for G4 rare gas rather
   than assuming it is offered. Also check current event and other in-game
   rewards; availability and quantities change.

The workbook's instructions describe raw building costs for the *next* Ops
assuming perfect progression, before personal efficiencies. A previously
reported **4,000 rare G4 gas for buildings from 42 to 43** could not be
independently verified from the accessible workbook export: cost labels are
not reliably exposed there. Do not budget from that number without checking
the current in-game building requirements and the full workbook view. Ship
and research spending is separate.

## Camping and breakpoint guidance

In the workbook, **Go** means little reason to hold back, **Slow** suggests
building or ship preparation, and **Camp** marks a major barrier or useful
stopping point. These are contributor judgments, not game unlocks.

| Ops | RevDeuce's workbook rating | Planning context |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 35 | Camp | Epic ship and specialty ship preparation. |
| 39 | Camp | Catch up on research and efficiencies. |
| 42 | Slow | G4 uncommon ship unlocks; prepare for the next step. |
| 43 | Slow | Rare G4 refinery access begins, per Scopely and the workbook. |
| 46 | Camp | G4 rare ship preparation. |
| 49 | Go | The sheet also calls the expensive 49→50 transition a “forced camp”; budget time and materials. |
| 50 | Camp | An optional place to finish G4 progression. |

These are RevDeuce's individual entries in the
[Master Chart](https://docs.google.com/spreadsheets/d/1nKsUSGGTa0Q2GW4YeAmiQVOiHSJnmfyu54E0Y9n_keg/edit?gid=495226859#gid=495226859),
not the aggregate: for example, JulesVern marks Ops 46 Go, and
BlueMandalorian marks Ops 49 Camp. Read their reasoning alongside your own
resources and objectives.
