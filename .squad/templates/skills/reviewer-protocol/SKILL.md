## Review and revision

Data independently reviews the current substantive revision for correctness,
security, privacy, safety, and test quality. A blocking finding must be resolved
or explicitly accepted by an authorized human before merge. The original author
may address feedback unless the reviewer explicitly requests another owner or
the task requires different expertise. The reviewer must independently review
the revised substantive changes; an earlier approval does not cover later edits.
Preserve required approvals and branch protections.
